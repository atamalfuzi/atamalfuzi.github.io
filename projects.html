Open work

Personal projects built from publicly available data. They are not associated with my work for any current or past employer and do not represent their views.

  • BC's electricity balance: the drought, imports and demand

    Open data analysis in Python, with interactive charts and a monthly dashboard, 2026

    The 2023 drought was a once-in-100-years low on the Fraser, and low inflows explain most of the 11 TWh fall in BC's hydro output. Clustering, a regime model and machine learning trace how trade with the US moved hour by hour, and demand is forecast six months out with an average error of 3.8%, with calibrated ranges.

    Project pageDashboardCode

  • Household gas demand in Canada since 1981: price, efficiency and forecasts

    Open data analysis in Python, with interactive charts and a monthly dashboard, 2026

    A 10% higher gas price cuts BC households' use by about 1.2% in the first year, and two common traps overstate that. Partial pooling across six provinces, heating-curve models and machine learning show where the long fall in use came from, and a year's demand is forecast to within 5-7%, with calibrated ranges.

    Project pageDashboardCode

  • Rent and mortgage burden in BC cities

    Open data analysis in R, 2026

    Rent's share of family income rose in 23 of 25 BC urban areas between 2008 and 2023.

  • Population change in BC municipalities

    Open data maps in R, 2024, updated 2026

    Percent change in population for every municipality, from 2003 to 2023 and projected to 2046. 147 of 191 areas grew; 66 are projected to shrink.
