Energy Strategy Reviews September 17, 2025

Analyzing the consequences of postponing electricity subsidy reforms in Iran: A retrospective study

Applied state-space modelling (Kalman filter) and ARDL techniques to estimate time-varying electricity demand elasticities and assess counterfactual reform scenarios spanning 1995–2015. Demonstrated how delaying subsidy reforms reduces consumer responsiveness, raises fiscal burdens, and undermines long-run policy effectiveness — highlighting the case for phased reforms with targeted support for vulnerable households.

Authors: Siab Mamipour · Mahsa Ghahremani · Ata Malfuzi · Seyed Armin Motahar

Energy May 13, 2020

Economic viability of bitcoin mining using a renewable-based SOFC power system to supply the electrical power demand

Conducted financial analysis of bitcoin mining operations across 3 power system configurations, 4 pricing scenarios, and 11 countries to identify optimal conditions. Initiated collaboration with a multinational research team to gather, model, and analyse energy market data — producing 132 business plan scenarios. Stress-tested using price and policy change scenarios to validate robustness of findings.

Authors: Ata Malfuzi (first author) · Ali Saberi Mehr · Marc A. Rosen · Majed Alharthi · Anastasia A. Kurilova
