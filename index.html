Economic models and data analysis for public policy

I'm an economist and data scientist. I lead passenger transport modelling at Environment and Climate Change Canada and work as a macro modeller at the SESIT Lab, University of Victoria. Most of my work is on energy, climate and housing, in R, Python, Julia and SQL.
