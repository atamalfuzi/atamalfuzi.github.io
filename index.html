Energy Strategy Reviews September 2025

Analyzing the consequences of postponing electricity subsidy reforms in Iran: A retrospective study

Applied state-space modelling (Kalman filter) and ARDL techniques to estimate time-varying electricity demand elasticities and assess counterfactual reform scenarios (1995–2015). Demonstrated how delaying subsidy reforms reduces consumer responsiveness, raises fiscal burdens, and undermines policy effectiveness.
