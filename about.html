Bio

I'm an economist and data scientist. On most projects I do the whole job: define the question, clean and combine the data, build and test the model, and write up the findings for the people who need them.

My technical work covers machine learning, applied econometrics and energy-economic modelling, in reproducible R, Python, Julia and SQL code. I've built analytical tools used in the federal government, contributed to open-source energy models and published peer-reviewed research in energy economics.

At Environment and Climate Change Canada I lead transportation electrification research as Passenger Transport Lead. In parallel I work as a Macro Modeller with the SESIT Lab at the University of Victoria, endogenising carbon markets in the open-source CIMS model from SFU.
