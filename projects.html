BC Housing Affordability from Open Data

Rent and mortgage payments as a share of median family income across British Columbia's cities, built entirely from open Statistics Canada, CMHC and BC property transfer data. Rent's share of income rose in 23 of 25 urban areas between 2008 and 2023, and the 2022–23 rate increases pushed ownership costs past 40% of income in Vancouver, Victoria and Kelowna.
