Papers

  • Analyzing the consequences of postponing electricity subsidy reforms in Iran: A retrospective study

    Energy Strategy Reviews, 2025. Siab Mamipour, Mahsa Ghahremani, Ata Malfuzi, Seyed Armin Motahar.

    State-space modelling (Kalman filter) and ARDL estimates of time-varying electricity demand elasticities, with counterfactual reform scenarios for 1995-2015. Delaying subsidy reform made consumers less responsive to prices and raised the fiscal cost, which supports phasing reforms in with targeted help for vulnerable households.

  • Economic viability of bitcoin mining using a renewable-based SOFC power system to supply the electrical power demand

    Energy, 2020. Ata Malfuzi (first author), Ali Saberi Mehr, Marc A. Rosen, Majed Alharthi, Anastasia A. Kurilova.

    Financial analysis of bitcoin mining across 3 power system configurations, 4 pricing scenarios and 11 countries, done with a multinational research team. The 132 business-plan scenarios were stress-tested against price and policy changes.
