Bio

I'm a data scientist and economist who works end-to-end on quantitative projects — defining the question, integrating and cleaning data, building and validating models, and communicating findings through clear briefings and reports.

My technical work spans machine learning, applied econometrics, and energy-economic modelling, with a strong emphasis on reproducible workflows using R, Python, Julia, and SQL. I've built production analytical tools for federal government, contributed to open-source energy models, and published peer-reviewed research in energy economics.

At Environment and Climate Change Canada I lead transportation electrification research as Passenger Transport Lead. In parallel I work as a Macro Modeller with the SESIT Lab at the University of Victoria, endogenising carbon markets in the open-source CIMS model from SFU.
